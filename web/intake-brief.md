# Intake screen brief (Impeccable shape, Oct 26, 2026, confirmed as written)

Visual world: "The Notice, Answered". The plain-language notice register (Medicare Summary Notice, Florida Notice of Action). White bond, one federal blue band, ink text, gold highlighter only on fields to check, light hairline rules. One workhorse sans (Public Sans) at a real scale, body 18px on the phone. Generous density, one column. Signature move: the deadline as a round postmark stamp with the date and the day count, in blue ink; the only expressive element, borrowed from the "Certified Mail" alternate. Citations product-wide: document excerpts with the document title and page, never a bare footnote number. Intake itself shows no citations.

1. Job and audience. A patient, caregiver, or billing clerk with a denial letter in hand, stressed, on a phone. Route `/case/new`, reached from a short landing page at `/` that is its own surface, shaped later, inheriting this world.
2. Outcome and proof. A photo, PDF, or pasted text becomes a correct case card. Success is Confirm with every flagged field checked, the case opened, and the investigation started. Demonstration data is the Sunshine Health sample from the contract (web/README.md) and the synthetic letters. No real letters anywhere.
3. Scope. Production-ready in Next.js 16 App Router and Tailwind 4 under `web/src/app/case/new`. Untouched: `api/`, `policyqa/`, the contract. Anti-goals: no stepper chrome, no dashed drop zone as the hero, no illustration, no confidence percentages (the word Check does the job), no medical content.
4. States.
   - Uploading. Entry offers three equal ways: take a photo (camera capture), choose a PDF or image, paste text. After submit the letter stays on screen (thumbnail or text excerpt) while staged messages advance: reading the letter, finding the program, filling the card. Designed for up to 30 seconds with Cancel; at 30 seconds a timeout line with Retry. Errors: unreadable photo (retake), wrong type or size, text that is not a denial letter, including a treatment question, which gets the scope refusal and what the product does instead, and API unreachable.
   - Reviewing. A one-paragraph plain-language reading of the letter. Under "Check these details", the ten contract fields as label and editable value rows: program and age group as selects, dates and amount as typed inputs, the rest as text. Medium and low confidence rows carry the gold bar and Check. The postmark shows the claimed deadline and the day count from today; a missing deadline reads "No deadline found" and is flagged. "Show the letter as read" reveals the raw text. Confirm is a full-width button at the foot.
   - Confirmed. One tap: edits are sent, the card locks read-only, the investigation call fires, and the stamp lands. Until the investigation screen exists, the state shows the locked card and a "Case opened" line.
   - Ranges: raw text 200 to 6,000 characters; denial reason up to three sentences; service up to 80 characters; amount up to five figures; dates a single day or a range; program may be unknown.
5. Interaction and layout. One column, 16px gutters, 44px targets, primary action pinned at the foot on the phone. At tablet width the same column centers at about 640px; at desktop the letter preview sits beside the card. State changes step, nothing fades; reduced motion respected.
6. Constraints and decisions. English, plain language. Accessibility AA in practice. A fixture mode serves the sample case until the intake endpoint lands. Reusable pieces: CaseCard, FieldRow, DeadlineStamp, SourcePanel, ProgressStages. Decided: flagged fields must be edited or tapped "Looks right" before Confirm enables; the postmark stays blue ink and never turns red; desktop shows the letter beside the card.
