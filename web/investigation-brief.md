# Investigation screen brief (Oct 26, 2026, same shape as intake-brief.md)

Visual world: "The Notice, Answered", unchanged from intake. White bond, the one federal blue band, ink text, gold only on items to check (none on this screen), hairline rules, Public Sans, body 18px on the phone, one column, 44px targets. State changes step, nothing fades, reduced motion respected. Citations are document excerpts with the document title and page, never a bare footnote number; the quote is always one tap away.

1. Job and audience. The same person who confirmed the case card a moment ago, still on the phone, now waiting while the case works itself. Route `/case/[id]`, reached from Confirm on `/case/new`. The screen is the case's home: it loads the case by id, shows the investigation while it runs, and keeps the findings when it is done.
2. Outcome and proof. The confirmed case becomes a list of findings, each a question the agent asked the written rules, with the answer and the verified citations. Success is every planned question answered or honestly left unsettled, with the verification coverage, the cost, and the time shown at the foot. Demonstration data is the Sunshine Health sample: five findings including one not settled and one the verifier could not confirm.
3. Scope. Next.js 16 App Router and Tailwind 4 under `web/src/app/case/[id]`, pieces under `src/components/investigation/`, the client and types in `src/lib/case.ts` (existing exports untouched). Untouched: `api/`, `policyqa/`. Anti-goals: no spinner art, no progress bar, no confidence percentages, no verdict (that is the next screen), no medical content.
4. States.
   - Loading. One line: "Loading case c_sample1…".
   - Not found (404). One line and a link to start a new case.
   - Not started (status `intake`). The case card and a Start the investigation button; the POST is allowed only in this state.
   - Investigating. The locked case card compact at the top (payer, case id, service, the letter's reason, the postmark with the deadline and the day count; the ten rows behind "Show the full case card"). The route line in plain words: "Investigating under Florida Medicaid (MMA)"; a dual case reads "Investigating under Florida Medicaid and Medicare, side by side, because …". A stepped progress line "Question 3 of 6" (aria-live polite). Findings appear as they arrive, newest at the bottom, no fade. The screen polls `GET /case/{id}` every 1.5 seconds while `status` is `investigating` and `investigation.status` is `running`, and stops on `investigated` or `failed`.
   - Investigated. All findings. Each finding card: the topic as a small overline, the question as the heading, the answer in body text, then the citation chips (document title, page); each chip toggles the quoted excerpt inline. `supported`: the answer with at least one verified citation. `not_settled`: the label "The documents do not settle this" where the answer would be. This is abstention, a correct outcome, in the same register as an answer, never styled as an error. `unsupported`: the answer struck through, the ink label "Could not verify", and the struck quotes with the verifier's reason for each. Unsupported findings are struck out on screen, not hidden. Dual cases group findings by jurisdiction with the jurisdiction as the heading: side by side at desktop width, stacked on the phone.
   - Trust panel at the foot, once the investigation is done: verification coverage as "3 of 4 findings verified" (never a percentage), with a line counting findings not settled and findings struck; cost in dollars to four decimals; time in seconds; the model name; "Show the trace" toggles a plain list of spans with milliseconds when the API sends `trace`, and the toggle is absent when it does not.
   - Failed. A plain error section with the API's reason and Try again. Try again reloads the case; the investigation cannot be restarted once the case has left intake, and the screen says so in one line.
5. Interaction and layout. One column, 16px gutters, 44px targets. At tablet width the column centers at 640px. At desktop a dual case widens to 1120px with the two jurisdictions side by side; a single-jurisdiction case keeps the 640px column. The citation chip is a button with `aria-expanded`; the excerpt is a blockquote with the title and page as its caption. The progress line and the findings list are polite live regions.
6. Constraints and decisions. English, plain language, accessibility AA in practice: the struck state carries the words "Could not verify" and the abstention carries its sentence, so no state is color-only. Fixture mode (`NEXT_PUBLIC_INTAKE_MODE` unset or `fixture`) reveals one finding every 0.8 seconds from a canned set with synthetic quotes attributed to plausible document titles and pages, under the "Sample case" tag; a case id containing `dual` serves a dual-eligible sample, `c_fail` a failed investigation, and any other id is not found. Live mode calls the API at `NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL` with the 30-second timeout and `IntakeError` codes from intake, plus `not_found`. Decided: the intake screen navigates to `/case/{case_id}` as soon as the investigate POST returns, and keeps its "Case opened" line only when that POST fails; the postmark on this screen is the landed stamp; per-finding cost and time sit in a small muted line under each card so the trust panel's totals can be checked.

## Page viewer (Oct 26, 2026, added to this brief)

Purpose: a verified citation proves itself. Every expanded quote, in a finding, the verdict, the checklist, the deadline and filing lines, or inline in the appeal letter, carries a "See the page" button that shows the real PDF page as an image with the quoted lines highlighted in gold, and a line saying whether a vision model reading the highlighted area agrees with the quote.

- Layout. Phone and tablet: a full-width section directly below the quote. Desktop (1024px and up): a side panel to the right of the case column that stays in view (sticky) while the page scrolls; the case column keeps its width up to 640px beside it. One viewer is open at a time: opening another closes the first.
- Contents, top to bottom. The header line "{title}, page {n}"; Zoom (2x, centered on the highlight) and Close, both real 44px buttons; the page image at the panel's width with one gold box per highlighted line (`--gold` at 0.45 alpha, multiply blend), scaled from the API's pixel boxes to the rendered size; the vision line. On open the image scrolls so the first box sits in the top third of the panel.
- States. Loading: "Loading page {n} of {title}…". Ready: image and boxes. Not located (`found: false`): the page without boxes and the ink line "The quote was not located on this page image; the verifier matched the text layer." No PDF on the server (503 `no_pdf`): "The PDF for this document is not on this server. Open it at its source." with a link to the manifest URL when known. Unknown document (404), service unreachable, timeout (30 seconds): one plain line and Try again. Vision: "Checking the highlight with vision…", then "Vision check: the highlighted text reads “…” and matches the quote." or "… and does not match the quote." (ink, never red), or "Vision check unavailable: {error}". No vision check runs when the quote was not located.
- Motion. The panel steps in; the boxes draw left to right (150 ms width) only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`. Nothing fades.
- Accessibility. Side panel is `role="dialog"` with an `aria-label` (non-modal); the phone version is a plain section. Focus moves into the panel on open and back to the citation chip on close; Escape closes. Image alt: "Page {n} of {title}, with the cited text highlighted". Boxes are `aria-hidden`; the vision line is a polite live region. The gold is decorative; the words carry the meaning.
- Fixture mode. No network. Page images are synthetic stand-ins drawn from HTML with Playwright (`scripts/page-fixtures.mjs`), marked "SYNTHETIC FIXTURE PAGE" on the image, one per page the sample findings cite on the Florida documents (fl-04 pages 3 and 5, fl-21 pages 38, 41, 43), with boxes measured from the rendered text. fl-21 page 38 shows the not-located state (its citation is the fuzzy 0.93 overlap match); the federal documents (fed-02, fed-17, cited by the dual sample) show the no-PDF state with their manifest links. The vision check resolves after 1.2 s, agreeing, model `fixture`, its text prefixed "[synthetic fixture]".
- Live mode. `GET /doc/{doc_id}/page/{page}/highlight?quote=…&dpi=110`, then `POST /doc/{doc_id}/page/{page}/vision-check` with `{quote}`; the image is `{API}{image_url}`. Both calls abort when the viewer closes and time out at 30 seconds.
- Decided. Focus returns to the chip that opened the quote, not to the See the page button. A quote under 15 characters is not sent (the API answers 422) and shows a plain line. The viewer lives under the expanded quote, so collapsing the quote closes it.
