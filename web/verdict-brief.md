# Verdict and Action brief (Oct 26, 2026, same shape as intake-brief.md and investigation-brief.md)

Visual world: "The Notice, Answered", unchanged. White bond, the one federal blue band, ink text, gold only on items to check (none here), hairline rules, Public Sans, body 18px on the phone, one column, 44px targets. The postmark stays the one expressive element; the verdict headline borrows its register (ink, bold, the stamp's blue for an inconsistent denial) and its landing. Never red, never green. Citations are document excerpts with the title and page, the quote one tap away. No percentages anywhere.

1. Job and audience. The same person, still on `/case/[id]`, now reading the outcome: does the denial hold, and if not, what do they send, with what, by when, and where. The case page grows two sections under the findings: Verdict, then Action.
2. Outcome and proof. One of the three verdicts, verbatim: "The denial matches the policy.", "The denial appears inconsistent with the policy.", "The documents do not settle it." The rule quoted and cited, the gap between the letter's reason and the rule, and what would change it. The payer's best argument (the steelman) one tap away. For an applicable letter: the appeal letter with a verified citation after every claim sentence and unsupported sentences struck out on screen, the evidence checklist, the deadline with day count, and the filing path. Success is a letter the person can copy or download with no struck sentence in it.
3. Scope. `src/components/verdict/`, `src/components/letter/`, `InvestigationView.tsx` growing, types and fixture in `src/lib/case.ts` (existing exports keep working). Untouched: `api/`, `policyqa/`. Anti-goals: no score, no percentage, no confidence meter, no red or green, no medical content, no hidden unsupported text.
4. States.
   - Stage line, under the investigation line, a polite live region, stepped: "Weighing the payer's side…" (`deciding`), "Verdict reached." (`decided`), "Drafting the appeal letter…" (`drafting`), "Appeal letter ready." (`drafted`; when no letter applies, "Verdict reached. No appeal letter was drafted."). The page polls every 1.5 s until `drafted` or `failed`.
   - Verdict (`verdict.status` done). Overline VERDICT, heading "What the documents say", the headline as the biggest text on the page: inconsistent in the stamp's blue ink, consistent in ink, not settled in muted ink. Then "The rule" with chips, "The gap", "What would change it" when present, and the collapsed "The payer's best argument". A rule or steelman marked unsupported is struck with "Could not verify".
   - Action, letter applicable. Overline ACTION, heading "Your appeal letter": a framed letter block, To and Re, paragraphs of sentences; a claim sentence carries an inline chip (title, page; quote on toggle); a struck or unverified claim is struck with "Could not verify". "Copy the letter" (plain text, struck sentences left out, "[cite: title, p. N]" after verified claims; "Copied." confirms) and "Download as text".
   - Action, letter not applicable. "Why the denial holds" (consistent) or "Why no letter was drafted" (not settled) with the explanation, and "What would have made it covered" from the verdict.
   - Evidence checklist, "What to include with your appeal": a list with a local tick, each item with its reason and a chip when cited.
   - Deadline and filing. The postmark with the letter's deadline and day count and its note; "No deadline found" when the source is unknown; a chip when cited. "Where to file" and "How", each with a chip when cited.
   - Trust panel. Adds "{verified} of {claims} letter sentences verified; {struck} struck" and adds the verdict and letter cost and time to the case totals, with the split shown.
   - Failed. The stage that failed names itself ("The verdict could not be reached", "The appeal letter could not be drafted") with the API's reason; everything above stays.
5. Interaction and layout. One column at 640px; a dual case keeps 1120px for findings only. Chips are buttons with `aria-expanded`; inline letter chips keep a 44px hit area without breaking the line. Copy announces through a status line. Motion, only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`: the headline stamps in (150 ms, scale 1.04 to 1 out of a slight tilt), new findings and sections land (120 ms, 6px up), checklist ticks step. No opacity fades on text.
6. Constraints and decisions. Fixture mode: `c_sample1` inconsistent with an applicable three-paragraph letter, one struck claim, five checklist items, the deadline from the letter (Dec 13, 2026), filing from the appeal finding; `c_dual1` not settled with no letter; `c_consistent1` consistent with the explanation and no letter. Fixture quotes carry "[synthetic fixture quote, not from the document] ", model `fixture`, cost 0. Decided: the three headlines are rendered from `outcome`, verbatim, so wording cannot drift; a claim sentence that is neither verified nor struck is treated as struck; live Download links to `GET /case/{id}/letter.txt`.
